## proposal of developments
### example_Proposal0(easy/efficient): limit 1s(Trues) to 10,000.(use original score)
### Ci_Proposal1(moderate): limit 1s(Trues) to 10,000.(in attackCi/ use hungarian algorithm)
### Di_Proposal2(easy): make threshold (confidence of AttackDi) higher.
### Ci_Di_Proposal3(moderate): introduce noise step in attack(let 1/0 answers in Ci/Di) and weight them by quantity of noise
### Di_Proposal4(hard/scratch/efficient): explore all paterns of ML models(run learning on our code) and compare the target ML model.  
### Ci_Proposal5(moderate/efficient): make Mutual NearestNeighbors(original: Ci to Ai nearest only, introduce Ai to Ci nearest)
### Ci_Proposal6(moderate/scratch/efficient): extend NearestNeighbors to k(original: 1-NN only)


## development
### Ci_attack: extend output record(only 1-NN to k-NN)
### Di_attack: change output(result(1/0) to original "Confidence" value or best n Confidence)
### example_1: weight Ci_attack num_k values and Di_attack num_n values
### example_2: Ci_attack==1 "and" Di_attack==1
### example: output 10,000-ranked records

### new_attackDi_Ci: score num_n Di_attack answers by Ci_attack distance
### new_attackDi_Ci: output: 1st-ranked record
